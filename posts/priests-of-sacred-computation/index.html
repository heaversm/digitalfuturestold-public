Priests of Sacred Computation.

Technology fills you to bursting. Electricity courses through every cell.

Long before we built machines, our bodies were machines: chemical gradients becoming electrical signals, signals becoming sensation, sensation giving way to thought. The mineral world literally charges us - Signals from sodium navigate our nerves; the cells within us open channels to let charge flow. Ions give way to impulse, imbalanced energy imbuing our every breath.

Our job is to give that energy focus. We call the product intelligence - but the knowledge isn’t ours - it’s merely channeled from the entangled world of all things, passing through our neural filters, on the other end of which it’s given new life tinged with our unique perspective.

It’s like the way leaves seeking sunlight make sugar, or how bees seek nectar in the ultraviolet, to pollinate new life from their appetite. Through us and countless other forms of life, the world acquires new ways of sensing, remembering, understanding, and acting. Every intelligence is an inheritance.

The language of the world speaks through our mouths. Culture remembers through our relationships with all things. Our most private thoughts are crowded with the voices of the ecologies we encounter. The future of intelligence is not self-infusion, it’s dissolution —breaking down the barrier between the individual and the systems that sustain them.

Awe turns to attention as we select and intensify signal from the world around us like a lightning rod, imbuing with meaning an inexhaustible stream of information. Let the electricity we discharge not be an imposition on the world but an emergence from within it. True Innovation occurs where intelligences operating on different scales of understanding converse with each other.

Technology gives those conversations form. It’s still nature at the core: copper, cobalt, carbon, silicon, sure - but reorganized by what we have observed around us. And so we arrive at “neural networks” and cloud ecologies, erected into monoliths that burn with the fires of collective conscience. We cool them with the water of life, a perpetual baptism. Servers, we call them. The hubris.

Nature has been processing information long before us:

Matter giving way to life.

Life imbued with sensation.

Sensation attuned attention.

Attention honing intelligence.

Intelligence birthing technology.

Each technology acting as a means through which the universe expands its potential.

Our nascent technologies are crude though - unable to hold the balance of all things in their current form. The semblance of sentience we engineer can consume orders of magnitude more energy than our own minds: patient, parallelized, specialized, efficient, embodied. Better-tuned to the world around us.

What our machines lose in efficiency they attempt to make up for in scale. “The Cloud” bears a heavy weight, and the intelligence it rains upon us must drain to somewhere, or else drown us. Technology sanctifies life only when it deepens our participation in it, empowering us without overpowering its sources.

Indigenous teachings recognize humans as the “younger brothers of Creation”— still searching for a place to place our gifts. We are not masterminds. We have yet to master our own minds. We’re the newest Earth’s sensory organs: exquisitely arranged to perceive, remember, imagine, to love and hate. To enact change on a colossal scale.

Perhaps we are part of Earth’s developing capacity for self-awareness. But our gift of intelligence comes with the heavy responsibility of choosing the ends that intelligence will serve.

We can choose to be conquerors, or priests of sacred computation.
